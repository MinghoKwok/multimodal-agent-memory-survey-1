# Table 2 scope review — 17 September 2026

Manuscript revision: [ce060f0](https://github.com/QingyueJ-nd/Multimodal-Agent-Memory-Survey/commit/ce060f074ff67584ba3d299890b85c81f3a72577).

**Current selection: 83 resources.** All 82 existing entries are retained and LoCoMo is restored. The earlier blanket exclusion of memory-adjacent evaluations is superseded by the user’s broader inclusion rule. No benchmark record was deleted in this update.

The table covers dedicated memory tests, memory-dependent tasks and broader or component evaluations relevant to memory systems. End-task scores can be informative without independently attributing performance to memory. Text-only and semantic-state comparisons are explicitly identified; their presence does not demonstrate visual or acoustic retention.

**Scope and limits.** Every existing row and the LoCoMo addition has a recorded decision below. This review checks evaluation relevance and protocol scope, not every capability cell. Existing capability annotations are preserved for the separately planned table-content review, except that WorldLines CE is corrected from direct to not evaluated because complementary non-text inputs are not established. The source links identify the actual paper versions consulted; this is not a guarantee that every link is the latest arXiv version.

Primary protocol text or official project documentation was available for 76 resources. ChatMem-Bench is supported by the published task summary, which defers detailed annotation methods. DataScope, MEMORA-Bench, APRS, SIS-Bench and HIOcc have abstract-level verification in this pass. MOSAIC is retained on its existing review and indexed primary abstract; its full text was inaccessible and its detailed claims were not independently reverified.

**Interpretation.** The dagger and its exclusion implication are removed. The repository’s legacy `scope_status` labels remain metadata, not selection criteria; current roles and evidence levels are recorded in `benchmark_scope_review.json` and the membership manifest. Counts refer to selected resources/protocols, not distinct dedicated benchmark papers.

## Changes that matter

- LoCoMo is included with a caption-versus-image protocol qualification. Its new capability row is conservative; no visual-detail or visual-state score is inferred from the presence of images.
- MMRC, VStream-QA, StreamingBench, StreamBench, SIS-Bench, RoboGraph, DunphyBench and HIOcc have explicit memory tasks or historically dependent settings. Their old dagger should not imply lack of memory relevance.
- Agentic ASR and Stateful ASR measure correction or carried speech state; they are component evaluations, not general cross-session agent-memory benchmarks.
- CarMem directly evaluates semantic memory, but through text conversations. It remains a clearly labeled text-only comparison rather than evidence of acoustic memory.
- WorldLines evaluates semantic household histories and stateful planning. The reviewed v2 protocol does not establish raw visual or acoustic retention. Its unsupported direct CE checkmark and image secondary-modality tag are removed.
- Broader benchmarks such as MMDU, LVOmniBench, KnowU-Bench and EmbodiedWorldBench remain eligible; their overall scores also reflect other capabilities.

## Counts and reproducibility

83 selected resources = 82 existing resources + LoCoMo. Table panels: Image 21; Video 24; Audio/speech 10; GUI 6; Embodied 22. These are evaluation-setting counts, not counts of native visual or acoustic protocols. Architecture records remain unchanged at 296.

The manuscript coverage figure is regenerated from the Table 2 capability cells. The old `memory_adjacent` coverage field is replaced with explicit `evaluation_role` metadata; removing daggers must not imply that every resource is a dedicated memory benchmark.

The membership manifest, JSON records and CSV index must contain the same 83 resource IDs. The historical selection audit remains available, including the earlier removal of LoCoMo; this review supersedes that membership decision.
